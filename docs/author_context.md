## Repository Purpose & Research Philosophy
The primary goal of `PaddyMathPapers` is **educational, exploratory, and open**:
- **A Mathematical Sandbox:** It serves as an open computational laboratory to explore ideas developed over years, blending concepts across differential geometry, Lie groups, algebraic information theory, and quantum mechanics.
- **Active Learning Through Formalization:** The author works through theoretical concepts by writing rigorous proofs, developing simulation scripts in Python, and validating physical/numerical analogues.
- **Publication Intent:** While publication in academic venues (e.g., *ACM Transactions on Graphics*, *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing*) is an attractive long-term possibility, the immediate driver is deep learning, mathematical discovery, and building robust geometric and information-theoretic tooling.
  - **Paper 1 (Spectral Compression of 3D Surfaces):** Shows direct practical potential for geometric CAD, robotics, and graphics compression.
  - **Paper 2 (Spectral Entropy of Sequences):** A rich pedagogical and theoretical exploration bridging finite fields, continuous optimization landscapes, and quantum transport analogies (Anderson localization).

## Evolution of AI Collaboration & Provenance Tracking

> [!NOTE]
> **A Candid Note on AI Lineage:**
> Systematic prompt and provenance tracking (`.ai-lineage/` and `PROVENANCE.md`) was established after the repository was already underway. During the earliest exploratory phases of this project, interactions with AI models were not formally logged because the author was still developing and refining his AI workflow. As the author's mastery of AI agent orchestration matured, formal logging, commit attribution, and provenance ledgers were introduced as standard research practices. Today, all ongoing inquiries maintain full provenance tracking.

The author treats frontier AI models (Gemini, Claude, GPT) not as automated answer generators, but as **intellectual sparring partners and high-speed analytical assistants**:
1. **Human Direction (Principal Investigator):** All core geometric intuition, physical analogies (such as mapping residues to delta-potential wells on quantum rings), problem framing, and architecture originate with the human author.
2. **AI Assistance (Formalization & Exploration):** AI is utilized for expanding symbolic derivations, scaffolding LaTeX proofs, identifying edge cases (e.g., the prime-square deficit decay rate), surveying adjacent mathematical literature, and drafting vectorized numerical verification code.
3. **Verification & Auditability:** Every mathematical claim is subjected to numerical simulation in Python to verify validity before being incorporated into the manuscripts.
